Point of view

Security in the North has always been about reading the landscape. Today that landscape is written in regulation: DORA, the EBA guidelines, MiCA, and the expectations of a supervisor who will one day ask to see the evidence.

We have sat through those inspections. We know the difference between a framework that exists on paper and one that holds when the systems go dark, and we help boards own the second kind.
