Point of view

Security in the North has always been about reading the landscape. For decades that landscape was networks and people. Now it includes machines that write, reason and act. Every AI system a company deploys is another path into the forest.

We use machine intelligence the way a tracker uses a good pair of binoculars: to see further, not to walk for us. Our models surface the faint signals. Our operators, each with more than a decade in the field, decide what they mean.
